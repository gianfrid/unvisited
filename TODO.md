## Recording view fixes (from a real test ride)

1. Follow-camera framing: keep the fix centered horizontally but offset it
   below the vertical center (room to see ahead in the direction of
   travel), and enforce a minimum follow zoom so the view is not too
   zoomed out.
3. Live trace age fade: older parts of the recording track fade gradually
   and are almost transparent after a few minutes, so retreading it stays
   readable and direction is clear.
