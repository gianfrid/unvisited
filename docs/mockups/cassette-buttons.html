Cassette-deck button style — mockup (round 2)

Round 2: the keys float directly on the map — no bar/panel behind them — and REC and Pause are one key (the flat style's single record slot): raised red REC while idle, latched down reading Pause with the LED blinking while recording, latched reading Resume with a steady LED while paused. The replay scrubber is dressed as a transport control: a recessed groove with the played length tinted and a capstan thumb. The first bank is live — click the key: it starts, pauses, resumes; Stop stops.
