Cassette-deck button style — mockup

The record cluster as a vertical bank of transport keys (a phone's map edge), in the three states of a session. A latched key stays sunk while its function is engaged; the REC LED blinks while recording and goes steady while paused (deck-standby convention). The first deck is live — click the keys: REC starts recording, PAUSE pauses/resumes, STOP stops.
